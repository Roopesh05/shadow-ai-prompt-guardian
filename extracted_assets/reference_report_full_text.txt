ABSTRACT
VoxentraAI is an AI-powered citizen complaint management and emergency response system
designed to provide a centralized platform for reporting local issues through voice and text.
Citizens can report problems such as accidents, theft, damaged roads, water issues, electricity
failures, garbage accumulation, drainage problems, streetlight failures, public safety concerns,
and other emergencies. The system converts voice input into text using speech-to-text tech-
nology and uses artificial intelligence to understand the complaint. VoxentraAI automatically
classifies complaints, extracts important information such as complaint description and loca-
tion, detects the priority level, and asks the user for clarification when the provided information
is unclear. After confirmation, the system registers the complaint with a unique complaint ID
and automatically routes it to the appropriate department. The system also provides complaint
status monitoring, follow-up notifications, escalation of overdue complaints, suspicious com-
plaint handling, and AI-generated responses. The frontend of VoxentraAI is developed using
React, TypeScript, TSX, Vite, CSS, and Node.js. The backend is developed using Python,
FastAPI, and Uvicorn. PostgreSQL is used for database management. The system aims to
improve communication between citizens and authorities and support faster complaint handling
and emergency response.

Chapter 1
Introduction
1.1
Background
Citizens often face difficulties in reporting local problems to the appropriate authorities. Is-
sues such as damaged roads, water leakage, electricity failures, garbage accumulation, theft,
accidents, and other emergencies may require immediate attention. However, the absence of a
centralized intelligent platform can result in delayed reporting and slow response.
1.2
Project Overview
VoxentraAI is an AI-powered voice and text based citizen complaint management system. The
system allows citizens to report complaints through voice or text. The complaint is processed
using artificial intelligence to understand the problem, classify it, extract important information,
detect priority, and route it to the appropriate department.
1.3
Motivation
The main motivation behind VoxentraAI is to simplify the process of reporting local issues.
Citizens should not have to determine which department is responsible for a particular problem.
The system uses AI to understand the complaint and automatically assist in routing it to the
appropriate authority.
1.4
Objectives
The main objectives of VoxentraAI are:
• To provide a centralized platform for citizen complaints.
• To allow complaint reporting through voice and text.
• To automatically understand and classify complaints using AI.
• To extract important information such as location and complaint description.
• To detect the priority level of complaints.
• To automatically route complaints to the appropriate department.

• To provide complaint status monitoring.
• To provide follow-up and escalation for unresolved complaints.
• To generate AI-based responses to citizens.
• To improve communication between citizens and authorities.
1.5
Scope
The scope of VoxentraAI includes citizen complaint registration, voice input processing, speech-
to-text conversion, complaint classification, information extraction, location detection, priority
detection, department routing, complaint monitoring, follow-up, escalation, administrative man-
agement, and reporting.

Chapter 2
Problem Identification
2.1
Existing Scenario
Citizens currently use different methods to report local problems and emergencies. They may
need to contact different departments, visit government offices, use separate applications, or
make phone calls. This makes the complaint reporting process difficult and time-consuming.
2.2
Existing System
The existing complaint reporting process mainly depends on manual reporting methods and
separate platforms. Citizens are often required to identify the appropriate department before
submitting a complaint. In many cases, complaints may not be properly classified or prioritized.
2.3
Existing System Drawbacks
The existing complaint reporting process has several drawbacks:
• No centralized complaint reporting platform.
• Difficulty in identifying the correct department.
• Manual complaint classification.
• Delay in emergency response.
• Limited voice-based reporting.
• Difficulty in tracking complaint status.
• Lack of automatic follow-up.
• Lack of intelligent priority detection.
2.4
Problem Statement
Citizens have no centralized intelligent platform to report local issues such as accidents, theft,
damaged roads, water problems, electricity failures, garbage accumulation, and emergencies.
This makes it difficult for authorities to receive, classify, prioritize, and respond to complaints
quickly.

• Web-based complaint portal.

The brainstorming process focused on combining different technologies to improve the com-
plaint reporting experience. Voice recognition, artificial intelligence, location services, database
management, and automated notifications were considered as important components of the pro-
posed system.

The proposed ideas were evaluated based on ease of use, accessibility, response speed, automa-
tion, scalability, and reliability.
• A mobile application provides convenient access but requires installation.
• A web portal provides easy access but may require users to type detailed complaints.
• A voice-based system improves accessibility and reduces typing effort.
• An AI-powered system can automatically understand, classify, prioritize, and route com-
plaints.
